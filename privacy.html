Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Student Tools handles information when you use this website and its free calculators and study tools.

Student Tools is designed to provide simple browser-based utilities without requiring users to create an account for the main tools.

Information You Enter Into Calculators

The calculators on Student Tools are designed to process the values you enter directly in your browser. Calculator inputs are not intentionally sent to a Student Tools server for processing.

You should still avoid entering passwords, financial information, private identification numbers or other sensitive information into website tools.

Theme Preference

Student Tools allows you to choose between light and dark display modes. Your selected theme preference may be stored in your browser's local storage on your own device so that the website can remember your display preference.

This theme preference is stored locally in your browser rather than being used as an account profile on Student Tools.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current website does not require an account or use cookies to process calculator inputs. Browser storage may be used for the website's theme preference.

If advertising, analytics, cookies or other third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy should be updated to explain the relevant services and information practices before or when those features are introduced.

Third-Party Services

Student Tools is hosted using GitHub Pages. Visiting a hosted website may involve technical information being handled by the hosting provider according to its own policies.

Student Tools does not intentionally collect calculator input values on its own server.

External Links

Student Tools may link to external websites or resources when they are useful to visitors. External websites have their own privacy policies and terms, and Student Tools does not control their privacy practices.

Children and Students

The website provides general educational tools and does not require users to create an account to use the main calculators. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal or sensitive information through any website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, advertising, analytics or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the website's information practices, visit the Contact page.
