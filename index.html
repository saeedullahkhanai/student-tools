Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools free?

Yes. All the tools on this site are free to use.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign up or login.

Can I use these tools on mobile?

Yes. The site is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.

Are the calculations accurate?

The formulas are standard, but rules differ between schools and universities. Always confirm important results with your institution.

Do you store my entered information?

No. Calculations run in your browser and the numbers you enter are not sent to a server. Your theme choice is saved on your own device.
