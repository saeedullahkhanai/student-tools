How it works

Choose a category, enter a value and pick the units. The result updates as you type.

Formula

Result = Value × (From factor ÷ To factor)

Temperature: °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32, and K = °C + 273.15.

Example

5 km = 3.1069 mi. 98.6 °F = 37 °C.

Tips

  • Use Swap to reverse the conversion.
  • Very small or large results are shown to 8 significant digits.
  • More unit types may be added later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is it?

Conversion factors are exact international standards, shown rounded to 8 significant digits.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server.
