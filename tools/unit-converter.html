How to Use the Unit Converter

Select a category such as length, weight or temperature. Enter the number you want to convert, choose the original unit in the From field and choose the destination unit in the To field.

The result updates automatically as you enter or change the value. You can also use the Swap units button to reverse the conversion.

What Can You Convert?

This unit converter supports several common measurement categories useful for students, everyday calculations and general reference.

  • Length: convert units such as kilometers, miles and meters.
  • Weight: convert common units such as kilograms and pounds.
  • Temperature: convert between Celsius, Fahrenheit and other supported temperature units.

Unit Conversion Formula

Result = Value × (From factor ÷ To factor)

Length and weight conversions use standard conversion factors. Temperature conversions use temperature-specific formulas.

For example:

°F = (°C × 9 ÷ 5) + 32

Another common temperature conversion is:

K = °C + 273.15

Unit Converter Examples

Some example conversions include:

  • 5 km ≈ 3.1069 miles
  • 1 kilogram ≈ 2.20462 pounds
  • 98.6 °F = 37 °C

Why Use a Unit Converter?

Unit conversions are common in mathematics, science, engineering, assignments and everyday situations. A converter can help you quickly change a measurement into the unit required for a calculation or comparison.

Tips for Accurate Unit Conversion

  • Choose the correct measurement category before converting.
  • Check both the From and To units carefully.
  • Use the Swap button when you need the reverse conversion.
  • Remember that displayed results may be rounded for readability.
  • For academic or scientific work, follow the units and precision required by your course or assignment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I convert with this unit converter?

You can convert common units of length, weight and temperature, including units such as kilometers, miles, meters, kilograms, pounds, Celsius and Fahrenheit.

How does the unit converter work?

Choose a category, enter a value, select the unit you are converting from and select the unit you are converting to. The converted result is calculated in your browser.

Can I swap the units?

Yes. Use the Swap units button to reverse the From and To units and perform the conversion in the opposite direction.

How accurate are the conversions?

The converter uses standard conversion factors and displays results rounded for readability. The displayed result may therefore contain fewer digits than the underlying calculation.

Is my information saved?

No. The conversion is performed in your browser and the values you enter are not sent to a server by the calculator.
