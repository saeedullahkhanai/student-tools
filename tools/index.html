Free Online Student Tools

Student Tools provides a collection of simple online calculators and utilities designed to help students with common academic tasks. Whether you need to calculate your GPA, check attendance, estimate final exam marks or convert measurements, you can use the tools directly in your browser.

University and GPA Tools

University students can use the attendance, GPA, CGPA and GPA goal calculators to understand their academic results. The Final Exam Calculator can also help estimate the marks required to reach a target overall grade.

Study and Productivity Tools

The Study Timer helps organize focused study periods and breaks, while the Word Counter can help students check the length of essays, assignments and other written work.

Calculators and Utilities

The scientific, percentage, date and unit conversion tools are useful for mathematics, science, assignments and everyday calculations. Results are designed to be quick and easy to understand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What student tools are available?

Student Tools provides calculators and utilities for attendance, GPA, CGPA, grades, final exam marks, percentages, scientific calculations, date differences, unit conversion, word counting and study timing.

Are these student calculators free?

Yes. The calculators and study tools on Student Tools are available to use for free.

Can I use these tools on a phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on desktop computers, tablets and mobile devices.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The tools can be used without creating an account.

Are calculations performed in the browser?

The calculators are designed to perform their calculations in the browser, so you can enter values and receive results without needing an account.
