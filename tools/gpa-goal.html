How it works

Your CGPA is a credit-weighted average. Enter what you have so far, your target, and the credits you will take next. The calculator works out the average GPA you must earn in the coming credits.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How to Calculate CGPA.

Formula

Required GPA = (Target × (Completed + Next) − Current × Completed) ÷ Next

Example

Current CGPA 3.2 over 60 credits, target 3.3, next semester 15 credits: (3.3 × 75 − 3.2 × 60) ÷ 15 = 3.70. You need a 3.70 GPA this semester.

Tips

  • If the result is above the maximum scale, spread the goal across two or more semesters.
  • Higher credit courses move your CGPA more, so prioritise them.
  • Check whether repeated courses replace old grades at your institution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it "Not possible"?

The GPA you would need is higher than the maximum on your scale. Try a lower target or a longer timeline.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server.
