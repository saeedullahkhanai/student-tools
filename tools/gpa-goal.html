How to Calculate Your Required GPA

Your cumulative GPA is a credit-weighted average. To find the GPA you need to reach a target CGPA, enter your current CGPA, the credits you have already completed, your target CGPA, and the credits you plan to complete next.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How to Calculate CGPA .

Required GPA Formula

Required GPA = (Target CGPA × (Completed Credits + Next Credits) − Current CGPA × Completed Credits) ÷ Next Credits

The formula calculates the average GPA you must earn during your upcoming credits to reach the target CGPA.

GPA Goal Calculator Example

Suppose your current CGPA is 3.20 after completing 60 credits. Your target CGPA is 3.30, and you will complete 15 credits next semester.

Required GPA = (3.30 × 75 − 3.20 × 60) ÷ 15

Required GPA = 3.70

This means you would need approximately a 3.70 GPA over those 15 credits to reach a 3.30 CGPA, assuming a credit-weighted calculation.

What If the Required GPA Is Too High?

Sometimes the calculator may show that the required GPA is higher than the maximum GPA available on your grading scale. This means the target cannot be reached within the specified number of credits.

In that situation, you can try spreading your goal across two or more semesters. Completing more credits gives your CGPA more opportunities to move toward the target.

Tips for Reaching Your GPA Goal

  • Set a realistic target based on your current CGPA and remaining semesters.
  • Focus on courses with more credit hours because they have a larger effect on your credit-weighted GPA.
  • Check your university's official GPA and CGPA calculation policy.
  • Check whether repeated courses replace previous grades or are calculated differently at your institution.
  • If your required GPA is above the maximum scale, spread your target across multiple semesters.

GPA Goal Calculator FAQs

How does the GPA Goal Calculator work?

The calculator uses your current CGPA, completed credits, target CGPA and upcoming credits to calculate the GPA you need to earn in your upcoming credits.

What GPA do I need to reach my target CGPA?

Enter your current CGPA, completed credits, target CGPA and credits for your next semester. The calculator will determine the required GPA.

Why is my required GPA higher than the maximum GPA scale?

If the required GPA is higher than the maximum GPA available on your grading scale, the target cannot be reached in the specified number of credits. Try spreading the target across more semesters.

Does the GPA Goal Calculator save my information?

No. The calculation is performed in your browser and the information you enter is not sent to a server.

Can I use a 5.0 or 10.0 GPA scale?

Yes. You can enter the maximum GPA scale used by your institution, such as 4, 5 or 10.
