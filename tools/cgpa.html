How to Calculate CGPA

CGPA is calculated by combining the GPA and credit hours of multiple semesters. Each semester is weighted according to its total credits, so a semester with more credits has a greater effect on the final CGPA.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How to Calculate CGPA.

CGPA Formula

CGPA = Σ (Semester GPA × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

To calculate your cumulative GPA, multiply each semester GPA by its total credits, add all quality points, and divide the result by the total number of credits.

CGPA Example

Suppose a student has two semesters:

  • Semester 1: GPA 3.2 with 15 credits
  • Semester 2: GPA 3.6 with 18 credits

Quality points: (3.2 × 15) + (3.6 × 18) = 48 + 64.8 = 112.8

Total credits = 15 + 18 = 33

CGPA = 112.8 ÷ 33 = 3.42

CGPA vs GPA

GPA usually represents your academic performance for a single semester, while CGPA represents your cumulative academic performance across multiple semesters.

Tips for Calculating CGPA

  • Enter the correct GPA for each semester.
  • Enter the correct total credit hours for each semester.
  • Use the same grading scale for all semesters.
  • Check your university's official CGPA calculation policy.
  • Do not simply average semester GPAs if your university uses credit weighting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CGPA the same as GPA?

No. GPA usually represents one semester, while CGPA combines academic performance across multiple semesters.

How is CGPA calculated?

Multiply each semester GPA by its total credits, add the results, and divide by the total number of credits.

Does this calculator use credit weighting?

Yes. Each semester GPA is weighted according to the total credits entered for that semester.

Can I use this calculator for a 5.0 or 10.0 scale?

The calculation method can be used with different grading scales, but your semester GPAs must use the same scale consistently. Check your university's official grading policy.

Does the calculator save my information?

No. The calculation is performed in your browser and the information you enter is not sent to a server.
