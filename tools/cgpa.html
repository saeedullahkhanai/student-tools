How it works

Enter the GPA and total credits for every semester. Each semester is weighted by its credits.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How to Calculate CGPA.

Formula

CGPA = Σ (Semester GPA × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Example

Semester 1: GPA 3.2 with 15 credits. Semester 2: GPA 3.6 with 18 credits. (48 + 64.8) ÷ 33 = 3.42.

Tips

  • Use the same scale for every semester (4.0, 5.0 or 10).
  • Some institutions simply average semesters. Check which method yours uses.
  • Add a row for each semester using the button below the table.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CGPA the same as GPA?

GPA usually means one semester. CGPA covers all semesters together.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server.
