Study Timer

Study in focused sessions with short breaks using a simple Pomodoro-style timer.

How it works

Set how long you want to focus and how long to rest. Press Start, study until the timer ends, then take your break. A sound plays when each period finishes.

Formula

1 session = Focus time + Break time

A common pattern is 25 minutes of focus and 5 minutes of rest. After 4 sessions, take a longer break.

Example

With 25 minutes of focus and 5 minutes of break, four sessions take 2 hours including breaks.

Tips

  • Put your phone away during focus time.
  • Write down what you will study before you start.
  • Keep this tab open: the timer keeps correct time even if the tab is in the background.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the page is loaded. Nothing is sent to a server.

Why did it not start?

Focus and break times must be whole numbers between 1 and the shown limit.
