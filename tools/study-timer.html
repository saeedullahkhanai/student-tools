How to Use the Study Timer

Enter the amount of time you want to study and the length of your break. The timer starts with your focus period and then switches to break time when the focus session ends. Completed focus sessions are counted automatically.

A common starting point is 25 minutes of focused study followed by a 5-minute break. You can change these values depending on your subject, workload and study routine.

What Is the Pomodoro Study Method?

The Pomodoro method is a simple time-management technique that divides study into focused work periods followed by short breaks. The goal is to give your attention a clear task and a defined amount of time.

There is no single schedule that works for every student. You can use shorter or longer focus periods depending on your concentration and the type of work you are doing.

Study Timer Formula

1 study cycle = Focus time + Break time

For example, a 25-minute focus period followed by a 5-minute break gives a 30-minute study cycle.

Study Timer Example

Suppose you choose 25 minutes of focus time and 5 minutes of break time. One complete cycle takes:

25 minutes + 5 minutes = 30 minutes

If you complete four focus sessions, the total time including the four 5-minute breaks is approximately 2 hours.

How to Make the Most of a Study Timer

  • Choose one clear task before starting a focus session.
  • Remove unnecessary distractions from your study area.
  • Keep your phone away or silence unnecessary notifications.
  • Use the break to rest instead of starting another demanding task.
  • Record difficult topics so you can review them later.
  • Take a longer break after several completed sessions if needed.

Study Timer for Exams and Assignments

A study timer can be useful when preparing for exams, completing assignments, reading chapters, practicing programming or reviewing lecture notes. Breaking a large task into smaller timed sessions can make your study plan easier to manage.

For example, you could use one focus session for reading, another for solving practice questions and another for reviewing your notes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a study timer?

A study timer helps you divide study time into focused work periods and breaks. This page provides a simple Pomodoro-style timer that you can customize.

What is the Pomodoro study method?

The Pomodoro method is a time-management technique that uses focused study periods followed by short breaks. A common pattern is 25 minutes of focus followed by a 5-minute break.

Can I change the focus and break times?

Yes. You can set the focus time from 1 to 180 minutes and the break time from 1 to 60 minutes before starting the timer.

Does the study timer work offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, the timer can work without an internet connection because the timer runs in your browser.

Does the study timer save my study information?

No. The timer works in your browser and does not require an account or send your study session information to a server.
