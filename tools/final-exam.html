Final Exam Required Marks

How it works

Your final grade is the coursework (worth the remaining weight) plus the final exam. Enter your grade so far, how much the final is worth and the overall grade you want.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How Many Marks Do I Need in My Final Exam?.

Formula

Required % = (Target − Current × (100 − Weight) ÷ 100) ÷ (Weight ÷ 100)

Example

Grade so far 70%, final worth 40%, target 80%: (80 − 70 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = 95%. For a target of 60% you would need only 45%.

Tips

  • Use your pass mark as the target to see the minimum score you need.
  • Ask your teacher if the final has a minimum pass requirement of its own.
  • If the result is above 100%, ask about extra credit or re-weighting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I enter as "grade so far"?

The percentage you have earned on all assessments except the final exam, based on their own weights.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server.
