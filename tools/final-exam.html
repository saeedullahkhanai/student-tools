How to Calculate the Marks Needed in Your Final Exam

Your final course grade is based on the work you have already completed and the marks you earn in the final exam. The final exam calculator helps you determine the exam percentage required to reach a specific target grade.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How Many Marks Do I Need in My Final Exam? .

Final Exam Required Marks Formula

Required % = (Target − Current × (100 − Weight) ÷ 100) ÷ (Weight ÷ 100)

The formula uses your current grade, the percentage weight of the final exam and your target overall grade to determine the final exam score required.

Final Exam Calculator Example

Suppose your current grade is 70%, your final exam is worth 40% of the course, and you want an overall grade of 80%.

Required final exam mark:

(80 − 70 × 0.60) ÷ 0.40 = (80 − 42) ÷ 0.40 = 38 ÷ 0.40 = 95%

Therefore, you would need approximately 95% on the final exam to finish the course with an overall grade of 80%.

How to Use the Final Exam Calculator

  1. Enter your current grade before the final exam.
  2. Enter the percentage weight of your final exam.
  3. Enter the overall grade you want to achieve.
  4. Click Calculate.
  5. Use the result to understand the final exam score you need.

What If I Need More Than 100%?

If the calculator shows that you need more than 100%, your target grade cannot be reached through the final exam alone with the entered values. This can happen when your current grade is too low or the final exam does not have enough weight to raise your overall grade to the target.

In that situation, check your course policy for extra credit, additional assessments, grade replacement or other available options.

Using the Pass Mark as Your Target

You can use the passing percentage for your course as the target. This lets you estimate the minimum final exam score required to pass the course.

For example, if your institution requires 50% to pass, enter 50 as the target overall grade.

Tips for Getting an Accurate Result

  • Enter your actual current course percentage.
  • Check the official percentage weight of the final exam.
  • Use the correct target grade for your course.
  • Check whether your course has a separate minimum final-exam requirement.
  • Use the result as a planning estimate and confirm grading rules with your institution.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I enter as "grade so far"?

Enter the percentage you have earned from assessments completed before the final exam. It should represent your current average based on the completed coursework.

How do I calculate the marks needed in my final exam?

Enter your current grade, the final exam weight and your target overall grade. The calculator uses these values to determine the final exam percentage required to reach your target.

Can I use my pass mark as the target?

Yes. Enter your course's passing percentage as the target to estimate the minimum final exam score needed to pass.

What if the required mark is above 100%?

A result above 100% means your target cannot be reached through the final exam alone under the entered grading weights. Check your course policy for extra credit or other assessment options.

Does the final exam have to be the only remaining assessment?

This calculator assumes the final exam is the remaining assessment represented by the entered exam weight. If other assessments remain, include them according to your course's grading system or use the Grade Calculator for a more detailed weighted-grade calculation.

Does the Final Exam Calculator save my information?

No. The calculation is performed in your browser and the information you enter is not sent to a server.
