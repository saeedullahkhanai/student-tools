How it works

Type or paste text into the box. All counts update live. Your text never leaves your browser.

Formula

Reading time = Words ÷ 200 words per minute

Example

A 600-word essay takes about 3 minutes to read.

Tips

  • Check your assignment word limit before submitting.
  • Sentences are counted by . ! and ? marks.
  • Paragraphs are separated by a blank line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text stored?

No. Counting happens in your browser only.

How is reading time estimated?

At an average of 200 words per minute.
