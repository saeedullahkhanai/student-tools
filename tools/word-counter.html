How to Use the Word Counter

Type or paste your text into the text box. The tool automatically updates the word count, character count, sentence count, paragraph count and estimated reading time as you type.

This tool can be useful for students checking essays and assignments, writers checking article length, and anyone who needs a quick text statistics tool.

What Does the Word Counter Measure?

  • Words: Counts the words in your text.
  • Characters: Counts all characters, including spaces.
  • Characters without spaces: Counts characters after spaces are excluded.
  • Sentences: Counts sentences based on common sentence-ending punctuation.
  • Paragraphs: Counts separate blocks of text.
  • Reading time: Estimates how long the text may take to read.

Reading Time Formula

Reading Time = Words ÷ 200 Words Per Minute

The calculator uses an average reading speed of approximately 200 words per minute. Actual reading speed can vary depending on the reader and the difficulty of the text.

Word Counter Example

If your essay contains 600 words, the estimated reading time at 200 words per minute is approximately:

600 ÷ 200 = 3 minutes

Word Count for Essays and Assignments

Many school and university assignments have a required word limit. You can paste your assignment into this tool to quickly check the number of words before submitting it.

Remember that your institution may have specific rules about whether headings, references, quotations or other sections count toward the official word limit.

Characters With and Without Spaces

The total character count includes spaces, while the "Characters (no spaces)" count excludes spaces. This can be useful when a form or platform has a character limit.

Tips for Checking Your Text

  • Check your assignment word limit before submitting.
  • Paste the complete text if you want a more accurate total.
  • Use the character count when a website has a character limit.
  • Review the sentence count if you are checking writing structure.
  • Remember that reading time is only an estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

Type or paste text into the text box. The tool automatically calculates words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using an average reading speed of about 200 words per minute.

Does the word counter save my text?

No. Text counting is performed in your browser and the text you enter is not sent to a server.

Does it count characters without spaces?

Yes. The tool provides both the total character count and character count without spaces.

Can I use it for essays and assignments?

Yes. You can paste an essay, assignment, article or other text to check its word and character counts.
