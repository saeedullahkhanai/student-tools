How it works

Pick the calculator that matches your question, enter two numbers and press Calculate. Marks, discounts and growth all use the same three ideas.

Formula

X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y
Part ÷ Whole × 100 = Percentage
Change % = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100

Example

20% of 150 = 30. 45 out of 60 = 75%. From 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.

Tips

  • To get your exam percentage, use "X is what percent of Y" with marks and total marks.
  • Percentage change is measured against the old value.
  • Round only at the end to avoid small errors.
