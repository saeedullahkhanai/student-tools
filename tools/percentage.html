How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage shows a value as a part of 100. Percentage calculations are commonly used for exam marks, discounts, statistics, finance, grades and everyday calculations.

This page includes three useful percentage calculators: percentage of a number, part as a percentage of a whole, and percentage change.

Percentage Formulas

X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y
Percentage = Part ÷ Whole × 100
Percentage Change = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100

Percentage Calculator Examples

Example 1: Percentage of a Number

To calculate 20% of 150:

20 ÷ 100 × 150 = 30

Example 2: What Percent Is One Number of Another?

If you scored 45 marks out of 60:

45 ÷ 60 × 100 = 75%

Example 3: Percentage Increase

If a value increases from 80 to 100:

(100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 25% increase

How to Calculate Exam Percentage

To calculate your exam percentage, divide the marks you obtained by the total marks and multiply by 100.

Exam Percentage = Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks × 100

For example, if you score 420 marks out of 500:

420 ÷ 500 × 100 = 84%

Percentage Increase vs Percentage Decrease

Percentage change compares the new value with the original value. A positive result represents an increase, while a negative result represents a decrease.

Tips for Using the Percentage Calculator

  • Use the first calculator when you need a percentage of a number.
  • Use the second calculator to calculate exam or test percentages.
  • Use the third calculator to compare an old value with a new value.
  • Percentage change is measured against the original value.
  • Round your final answer only after completing the calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the percentage by the number and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 is 30.

How do I calculate what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 45 out of 60 is 75%.

How is percentage increase or decrease calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide the result by the old value, and multiply by 100.

Can I enter decimals?

Yes. The calculator accepts decimal values where they are mathematically meaningful.

Does the Percentage Calculator save my information?

No. The calculations are performed in your browser and the information you enter is not sent to a server.
