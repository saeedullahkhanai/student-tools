How to Calculate Your Overall Grade

A weighted grade combines different assignments, quizzes, tests and exams according to how much each one contributes to your course grade. Enter the score you earned and the percentage weight of each item.

The calculator multiplies each score by its weight and combines the results to calculate your overall percentage.

Weighted Grade Formula

Overall % = Σ (Score × Weight) ÷ Σ Weight

Here, Score is the percentage you earned on an assignment or exam, while Weight represents how much that item contributes to the overall course grade.

Grade Calculator Example

Suppose a course has the following grading components:

  • Assignment: 85% score with a 20% weight
  • Midterm: 70% score with a 30% weight
  • Final exam: 90% score with a 50% weight

Weighted scores: (85 × 0.20) + (70 × 0.30) + (90 × 0.50) = 17 + 21 + 45 = 83

Therefore, the overall course grade is 83%.

Using a typical grading scale, 83% would usually be a B. However, grading scales can vary by school, college or university.

How Weighted Grades Work

Not every assignment has the same importance. For example, a final exam may contribute 40% of your course grade while homework may contribute only 10%. A higher-weight assessment has a larger effect on your overall grade.

What If My Weights Do Not Add Up to 100%?

If you enter every grading component for the course, the weights should normally add up to 100%. If you are calculating your current standing using only completed assignments, quizzes or exams, the entered weights may represent only the completed work.

Typical Letter Grade Scale

  • A: 90% or above
  • B: 80%–89%
  • C: 70%–79%
  • D: 60%–69%
  • F: Below 60%

This is a common example grading scale. Your institution may use different percentage ranges or plus/minus grades such as A-, B+ or C+. Always check your course or university grading policy for the official scale.

Tips for Calculating Your Grade

  • Enter the actual percentage score for each assessment.
  • Enter the correct weight for every assignment, quiz, test or exam.
  • Check that all course weights are correct.
  • Use the calculator to estimate your current course standing.
  • Use the Final Exam Required Marks calculator to plan your target grade.
  • Check your university's official grading policy before making academic decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a weighted grade calculated?

A weighted grade is calculated by multiplying each score by its weight, adding the weighted scores together, and dividing by the total weight.

What is the formula for a weighted grade?

The formula is: Overall Percentage = Sum of (Score × Weight) divided by Sum of Weights.

What letter grade will I get?

This calculator uses a typical scale where 90% or above is A, 80% or above is B, 70% or above is C, 60% or above is D, and below 60% is F. Your institution may use a different scale.

Should my weights add up to 100%?

If you are entering all grading components for a course, the weights should normally add up to 100%. For current-standing calculations, you can enter only the completed components.

Can I use this calculator for assignments and exams?

Yes. You can add assignments, quizzes, tests, midterms, final exams or other graded components as long as you know their scores and weights.

Does the Grade Calculator save my information?

No. The calculation is performed in your browser and the information you enter is not sent to a server.
