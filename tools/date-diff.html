Days Between Dates

Find the number of days, weeks, months and years between two dates.

Result

Enter your numbers and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Advertisement

How it works

Pick two dates. The tool counts the full days between them and also gives weeks and a years-months-days breakdown. Useful for deadlines and exam countdowns.

Formula

Days = (End Date − Start Date) in days

Example

1 January 2026 to 1 March 2026 = 59 days, which is 8 weeks 3 days, or 2 months.

Tips

  • The end date itself is not counted; add 1 if you want both dates included.
  • If you enter the dates in reverse order they are swapped for you.
  • Dates are handled in UTC, so daylight saving does not affect results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it count weekends?

Yes. It counts every calendar day.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server.
