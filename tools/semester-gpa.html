How it works

Each course counts according to its credits. Multiply credits by the grade points, add everything up and divide by total credits.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How to Calculate GPA.

Formula

GPA = Σ (Credits × Grade Points) ÷ Σ Credits

Example

3 credits of A (4.0), 4 credits of B (3.0) and 3 credits of A- (3.7): (12 + 12 + 11.1) ÷ 10 = 3.51.

Tips

  • Leave unused rows empty; they are ignored.
  • Grade points differ between institutions, so check your own scale.
  • Use the CGPA Calculator to combine several semesters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work for a 10-point scale?

This tool uses the common 4.0 letter-grade scale. For other scales, use the CGPA Calculator and enter your GPA values directly.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server.
