How to Calculate Semester GPA

Your semester GPA is based on the credits and grade points of each course. To calculate it, multiply each course's credit hours by its grade points, add the quality points together, and divide the total by the number of credits.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How to Calculate GPA.

Semester GPA Formula

GPA = Σ (Credits × Grade Points) ÷ Σ Credits

In this formula, credits are the credit hours for each course and grade points are the numerical values assigned to your letter grades.

Semester GPA Example

Suppose you have three courses:

  • 3 credits with an A grade (4.0 grade points)
  • 4 credits with a B grade (3.0 grade points)
  • 3 credits with an A- grade (3.7 grade points)

Quality points: (3 × 4.0) + (4 × 3.0) + (3 × 3.7) = 12 + 12 + 11.1 = 35.1

Total credits = 3 + 4 + 3 = 10

Semester GPA = 35.1 ÷ 10 = 3.51

What Is a 4.0 GPA Scale?

A 4.0 GPA scale assigns numerical grade points to letter grades. For example, an A may be worth 4.0 points, while lower letter grades have lower grade-point values. The exact grading scale can vary between universities, so always check your institution's official grading policy.

Tips for Calculating Your GPA

  • Leave unused course rows empty; they are ignored.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a semester GPA?

Semester GPA is the grade-point average earned from the courses you complete during one academic semester. Courses with more credit hours have a greater effect on the final GPA.

How is semester GPA calculated?

Multiply each course's credits by its grade points, add the quality points, and divide by the total number of credits.

Does this calculator use a 4.0 scale?

Yes. This calculator uses a common 4.0 letter-grade scale. Grading systems can differ between institutions, so check your university's official grade-point scale.

Does the calculator save my information?

No. The calculation is performed in your browser and the information you enter is not sent to a server.

Can I calculate my CGPA from several semesters?

Yes. Use the CGPA Calculator to combine GPA results from multiple semesters.
