How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

Attendance percentage shows the proportion of classes you attended compared with the total number of classes held. Enter your total classes, classes attended, and required attendance percentage to calculate your current attendance and understand your attendance status.

Want a step-by-step explanation? Read How to Calculate Attendance Percentage .

Attendance Percentage Formula

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

For example, if you attended 36 out of 50 classes:

Attendance = (36 ÷ 50) × 100 = 72%

How Many Classes Can I Miss?

If your current attendance is above the required percentage, the calculator estimates how many future classes you can miss while keeping your attendance at or above the required level.

For example, if you attended 45 out of 50 classes and your required attendance is 75%, your current attendance is 90%. You can miss 10 more classes and still have 75% attendance:

45 ÷ (50 + 10) × 100 = 75%

How Many Classes Do I Need to Attend?

If your current attendance is below the required percentage, the calculator estimates how many consecutive classes you need to attend without missing any to reach your target.

For example, if you attended 36 out of 50 classes and your required attendance is 75%, your current attendance is 72%. You need to attend 6 consecutive classes:

(36 + 6) ÷ (50 + 6) × 100 = 75%

Tips for Maintaining Good Attendance

  • Keep a small buffer above your institution's minimum requirement.
  • Avoid unnecessary absences because early missed classes can have a larger effect on your percentage.
  • Check your university or department's official attendance policy.
  • Ask whether approved medical leave or other authorized absences are treated differently.
  • Use this calculator regularly to monitor your attendance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is attendance percentage calculated?

Attendance percentage is calculated by dividing the number of classes attended by the total number of classes and multiplying the result by 100.

How many classes can I miss?

Enter your total classes, classes attended, and required attendance percentage. The calculator will estimate how many future classes you can miss while staying at or above your required percentage.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach my target?

The calculator determines how many consecutive classes you need to attend without missing any in order to reach your required attendance percentage.

Does the attendance calculator save my information?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and the information you enter is not sent to a server.

Why does it say "Not possible"?

If your requirement is 100% and you have already missed a class, you cannot reach 100%, no matter how many additional classes you attend.

Does the calculator round the result?

The attendance percentage is shown to two decimal places. Class counts are whole numbers and are calculated in the safe direction.

Does every university use the same attendance rule?

No. Attendance policies differ between universities and institutions. Some institutions may have rounding rules, condonation policies, or approved absence rules. Always confirm your institution's official policy.
