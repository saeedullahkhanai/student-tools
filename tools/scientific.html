How it works

Type with the buttons or your keyboard. The result previews as you type and brackets close automatically. Press = to save the calculation in the history, then click any entry to reuse it. Choose Deg or Rad for angles.

Formula

Order of operations: brackets → functions and powers → × ÷ → + −

Examples of what you can type: 2×(3+4)^2, sin(30), √(144), 5!, log(1000).

Example

2 × (3 + 4)² = 98. In Deg mode sin(30) = 0.5. √(144) = 12 and 5! = 120.

Tips

  • Check the Deg/Rad setting before using sin, cos or tan.
  • % divides a number by 100, so 50 + 10% gives 50.1.
  • After pressing =, start with an operator to continue from the answer, or a number to start fresh.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to type any expression?

Yes. Expressions are parsed by the calculator itself, not run as code, and nothing leaves your browser.

Why do I see "Invalid input"?

The value is outside the allowed range, such as the square root of a negative number, ln(0) or a factorial of a decimal.

Is my data saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing you enter is sent to a server.
