How to Calculate GPA

Understand credits, grade points and how to work out your semester GPA.

What is GPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is the average of your grade points, weighted by the credit hours of each course. A 4-credit course counts more than a 2-credit course.

Grade points (common 4.0 scale)

Letter gradeGrade points
A4.0
A-3.7
B+3.3
B3.0
B-2.7
C+2.3
C2.0
C-1.7
D+1.3
D1.0
F0.0

Your institution may use different values, so check your grading policy.

The formula

GPA = Σ (Credits × Grade Points) ÷ Σ Credits

Step by step

  1. List each course with its credits and grade points.
  2. Multiply credits by grade points for each course.
  3. Add all those results together (quality points).
  4. Divide by the total credits.

Example

3 credits of A (4.0), 4 credits of B (3.0), 3 credits of A- (3.7): 12 + 12 + 11.1 = 35.1 quality points. Total credits = 10. GPA = 35.1 ÷ 10 = 3.51.

Tips

  • Do not average the grade points without credits unless every course has equal credits.
  • Courses graded pass/fail are usually left out.
