How Many Marks Do I Need in My Final Exam?

Learn how to calculate the final exam score needed to reach your target overall grade.

How the Final Exam Grade Is Calculated

Your final course grade usually combines the work you have completed so far with your final exam. The final exam has a specific percentage weight, such as 30%, 40% or 50%.

If you know your current grade, the final exam weight and your target overall grade, you can calculate the exam percentage you need.

The Formula

Overall = Grade So Far × (100 − Final Weight) ÷ 100 + Exam × Final Weight ÷ 100

Solving for the required exam score:

Exam % = (Target − Grade So Far × (100 − Weight) ÷ 100) ÷ (Weight ÷ 100)

Step-by-Step Calculation

  1. Find your current grade before the final exam.
  2. Find the percentage weight of the final exam.
  3. Choose your target overall grade.
  4. Put the values into the formula.
  5. Check whether the required exam score is realistically possible.

Worked Example

Suppose your grade so far is 70%, your final exam is worth 40%, and you want an overall grade of 80%.

Your coursework contributes:

70 × 0.60 = 42

You therefore need 38 percentage points from the final exam:

(80 − 42) ÷ 0.40 = 95%

You would need approximately 95% on the final exam to finish with an 80% overall grade.

Another Example

With the same 70% grade so far and a final worth 40%, if your target is 60%:

(60 − 42) ÷ 0.40 = 45%

You would need 45% on the final exam to reach a 60% overall grade.

What If the Required Score Is Above 100%?

If the calculation gives a required score greater than 100%, the target cannot be reached through the final exam alone under the current grading weights.

In that situation, check whether your course includes extra credit, assignments or other remaining assessments that can improve your overall grade.

Common Mistakes

  • Using your desired grade instead of your actual grade so far.
  • Confusing the final exam weight with the coursework weight.
  • Forgetting to convert percentages such as 40% into 0.40 when using the formula.
  • Assuming every course uses the same grading system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the marks needed in my final exam?

Enter your current grade, final exam weight and target overall grade into the formula or use the Final Exam Required Marks calculator.

What should I enter as my grade so far?

Enter the percentage you have earned from coursework completed before the final exam.

What if the required exam score is above 100%?

It means the target cannot be reached through the final exam alone with the current grades and weighting.

Can I use a passing grade as my target?

Yes. Enter the passing percentage required by your institution as your target overall grade.
