How Many Marks Do I Need in My Final Exam?

Work out the score you need in the final exam to reach your target grade.

The idea

Your overall grade combines your coursework and the final exam, each with its own weight. If you know the weight of the final and the grade you want, you can solve for the exam score.

The formula

Overall = Grade so far × (100 − Weight) ÷ 100 + Exam × Weight ÷ 100

Solving for the exam score:

Exam % = (Target − Grade so far × (100 − Weight) ÷ 100) ÷ (Weight ÷ 100)

Step by step

  1. Find your grade so far, based on everything except the final.
  2. Find the weight of the final exam (for example 40%).
  3. Choose your target overall grade.
  4. Put the numbers into the formula.

Example

Grade so far 70%, final worth 40%, target 80%: 70 × 0.6 = 42. (80 − 42) ÷ 0.4 = 95%. For a target of 60% you need (60 − 42) ÷ 0.4 = 45%.

If the answer is above 100%

It means the target cannot be reached with the exam alone. Choose a lower target, or ask whether extra credit is available.

Common mistakes

  • Using your overall grade instead of the grade without the final.
  • Mixing up the weight of the final with the weight of the coursework.
