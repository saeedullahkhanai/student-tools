How to Calculate CGPA

Learn how to calculate your cumulative GPA from multiple semesters using semester GPAs and credit hours.

What Is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents your overall academic performance across multiple semesters.

A semester GPA normally describes your performance during one semester, while CGPA combines your academic results from several semesters into one cumulative value.

When semesters have different numbers of credit hours, a credit-weighted calculation is commonly used because a semester with more credits has a greater effect on the overall result.

CGPA Formula

CGPA = Σ (Semester GPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

In simple words, multiply each semester's GPA by the total credits for that semester, add all the results, and then divide by the total number of credits.

How to Calculate CGPA Step by Step

  1. Write down the GPA for each completed semester.
  2. Write down the total credit hours for each semester.
  3. Multiply each semester GPA by its semester credits.
  4. Add all the resulting quality points.
  5. Add the credit hours from all semesters.
  6. Divide total quality points by total credits.
  7. Round the result according to your institution's grading policy.

CGPA Calculation Example

Suppose a student has completed two semesters:

  • Semester 1: GPA = 3.2, Credits = 15
  • Semester 2: GPA = 3.6, Credits = 18

First calculate the quality points:

Semester 1 = 3.2 × 15 = 48

Semester 2 = 3.6 × 18 = 64.8

Total quality points = 48 + 64.8 = 112.8

Total credits = 15 + 18 = 33

Therefore:

CGPA = 112.8 ÷ 33 = 3.42

The student's cumulative GPA is approximately 3.42.

Why Credit Weighting Matters

A simple average of semester GPAs does not always give the same result as a credit-weighted CGPA.

For example, the simple average of 3.2 and 3.6 is:

(3.2 + 3.6) ÷ 2 = 3.40

But the credit-weighted calculation gives approximately 3.42 because the second semester has 18 credits while the first semester has 15 credits.

This is why you should use the total credit hours for each semester when your institution uses a credit-weighted CGPA system.

CGPA vs GPA

GPA and CGPA are related but they usually describe different academic periods.

  • GPA: Usually represents your academic performance for a particular semester.
  • CGPA: Represents cumulative academic performance across multiple semesters.

For example, you might have a GPA of 3.60 in your current semester while your overall CGPA remains 3.42 after several semesters.

How to Calculate CGPA From More Than Two Semesters

The same method works whether you have two semesters or many semesters. Simply enter the GPA and total credits for every completed semester.

For example, if you have completed four semesters, calculate the quality points for all four semesters, add them together, and divide by the total credits from all four semesters.

CGPA to Percentage Conversion

CGPA-to-percentage conversion is not universal. Different universities, countries, and grading systems may use different conversion formulas.

Therefore, you should not automatically multiply your CGPA by a fixed number unless your institution officially specifies that method.

If you need to convert your CGPA into a percentage, check your university's official grading policy, transcript instructions, or examination regulations.

Common CGPA Calculation Mistakes

  • Taking a simple average of semester GPAs when credit weighting is required.
  • Entering incorrect semester credit hours.
  • Mixing GPA values from different grading scales.
  • Rounding each semester GPA too early.
  • Using an unofficial CGPA-to-percentage conversion formula.
  • Forgetting to include one or more completed semesters.

Tips for Getting an Accurate CGPA

  • Use the GPA shown on your official academic record when possible.
  • Verify the total credit hours for every semester.
  • Keep the same grading scale throughout the calculation.
  • Check your university's official CGPA policy.
  • Use the calculator to reduce manual calculation errors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CGPA?

CGPA, or Cumulative Grade Point Average, represents your overall academic performance across multiple semesters.

How is CGPA calculated?

Multiply each semester GPA by its total credits, add the resulting quality points, and divide by the total credits from all semesters.

Is CGPA the same as GPA?

No. GPA usually represents performance for a particular semester, while CGPA represents cumulative performance across multiple semesters.

Can CGPA be converted directly to percentage?

There is no single universal conversion formula. Conversion rules vary between institutions, so use your university's official policy.

No. The calculation is performed in your browser, and the information entered into the calculator is not sent to a server.
