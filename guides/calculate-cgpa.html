How to Calculate CGPA

Combine several semesters into one cumulative GPA, the right way.

What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is your overall GPA across all semesters. GPA usually describes one semester; CGPA covers everything so far.

The formula

CGPA = Σ (Semester GPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Step by step

  1. Write down each semester's GPA and total credits.
  2. Multiply each GPA by its credits.
  3. Add the results.
  4. Divide by the total credits of all semesters.

Example

Semester 1: GPA 3.2, 15 credits. Semester 2: GPA 3.6, 18 credits. (3.2 × 15 + 3.6 × 18) ÷ 33 = 112.8 ÷ 33 = 3.42.

Credit-weighted vs simple average

A simple average of 3.2 and 3.6 gives 3.4, slightly different from 3.42. Credit-weighted is the usual method, but some institutions average semesters equally, so check yours.

CGPA to percentage

Conversion rules differ a lot between institutions and countries. Use the formula published by your own university.
